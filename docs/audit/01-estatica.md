El constraint `no_overlapping_bookings` ([V1:86-89](file:///home/jjarias/Documents/UNIVERSIDAD/8vo%20semestre/Integrador-ii/SatoriPMS/apps/api/src/main/resources/db/migration/V1__initial_schema.sql#L86-L89)) utiliza `WHERE (status IN ('pending_payment', 'confirmed'))`.
